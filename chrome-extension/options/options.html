Where clips go

JotDrop Cloud

Checking...

  1. Open app.jotdrop.net and sign in (or create a free account).
  2. Open Devices & recovery, name the new device "Web clipper" and choose Create one-time pairing code.
  3. Copy the code, paste it below and choose an unlock passphrase for this browser.

The passphrase encrypts the cloud keys in this browser. It is not your account password and never leaves this computer. If you forget it, disconnect and pair again.

The clipper becomes one of your cloud devices. On the Free plan it counts toward the device limit.

Disconnect this browser

Removes the cloud keys and any unsent encrypted drafts from this browser. With the unlock passphrase (or while unlocked) the clipper also signs itself out of your cloud, which frees its device place. Your notes in the cloud stay.

Obsidian: background connection (optional)

No setup is required: the clipper works out of the box by opening Obsidian for every save. Connecting below is optional: it lets saves run quietly in the background without bringing Obsidian to the foreground.
