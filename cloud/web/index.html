Mail to JotDrop

Email anything to your own secret address and it becomes a note here, attachments included. Our server seals every incoming mail right away with a key that only your devices hold, so after that moment nobody but you can read it.

Mail to JotDrop is part of JotDrop Plus.

Anyone who knows this address can mail into your notes, so treat it like a key. Shared it by mistake? Make a new one; the old one stops at once.

A sender address can be faked, so this is a filter against noise, not proof of who really wrote a mail.

Ask JotDrop: AI with your own key

Ask JotDrop does what you ask in your notes, with your own AI account. Your question and the notes it needs go straight from this browser to the AI service you choose, never through JotDrop. "This week" sends a short digest of your notes, not the notes themselves. It is free on every plan; the AI service bills you for what you use.

The key stays in this browser, sealed with your workspace key. It is not synced, and JotDrop never receives it.
